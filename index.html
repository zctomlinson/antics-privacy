Antics

Privacy policy and support. Last updated: 7 November 2026.

Antics is an iPhone app for looking after pet ant colonies. It is made by Zachary Tomlinson.

Short version: Antics does not collect your data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

Privacy policy

What is stored, and where

Your colonies, care history, journal notes, journal photos and ant pets are saved only on your iPhone. Antics has no account, no server and no sign-in. We never receive your data and cannot see it.

What Antics does not do

  • It does not collect your name, email, location, contacts or any identifier.
  • It does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or any third-party code.
  • It does not track you, and it does not connect to the internet to send your data anywhere.

Permissions

  • Camera: only when you tap "Take a photo" in a journal entry, so you can photograph your colony. The photo is saved in that entry on your iPhone. If you say no, you can still pick photos from your library or write text-only entries.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on reminders. They are scheduled on your iPhone and never sent through a server.
  • Photo library: Antics only receives the photos you choose; it cannot browse your library.

Backups

"Export a backup" in Settings makes one file of your colonies and ant pets and opens the iPhone's share sheet. The file goes only where you send it (Files, AirDrop, Mail). Antics itself sends nothing. Your own iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone may also contain your Antics data; those are yours and are governed by Apple's terms.

Purchases

Antics Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Antics never sees your payment details. Antics only learns, from the App Store on your device, whether Pro is unlocked.

Deleting your data

Delete a journal entry, a colony or an ant pet (swipe) to remove it. Deleting the app removes everything Antics stored.

Children

Antics collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.

Support

Questions, a problem, or an idea for Antics? Email open_alfalfa6i@icloud.com. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you have, and what you were doing when it happened. Please don't send photos of your colony unless asked; they stay on your phone and are never needed to help.

Your data lives only on your iPhone, so if you change phones, use Settings > Export a backup first and Import from file on the new one.
